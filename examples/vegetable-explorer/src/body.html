Vegetable Explorer

Select a vegetable to see what it gives you and what it costs, and how it compares with 26 others sold in Spain. Nutrition is per 100 g of raw vegetable (USDA); prices are in € per kg, July 2026 (MAPA).

How the data and scores work
Nutrition
Approximate amounts per 100 g of raw vegetable, from USDA FoodData Central. The Nutrient Density Score (0 to 100) is the average of 12 nutrients: protein, fiber, vitamins A, C, E, K and B6, folate, potassium, magnesium, calcium and iron. Each nutrient is the share of the EU daily reference found in 100 g, capped at 100%. It measures a 100 g portion, not calories.
Prices
Average consumer prices in € per kilo, July 2026, from the MAPA panel (Spain's Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food). As bought is the price of the whole product. Edible part is the price of what you actually eat after trimming, peeling or shelling.
Per €1
How much of your daily needs the edible part of one euro of the vegetable covers, on average across the same 12 nutrients, each capped at 100%.

Sources and notes
  • Nutrition: approximate values per 100 g raw, USDA FoodData Central. They are provisional until Spain's BEDCA food database is imported, and vegetables eaten cooked will change.
  • Daily references: EU Regulation 1169/2011, Annex XIII. Fiber uses the EFSA reference of 25 g.
  • Prices: average Spanish consumer prices, July 2026, MAPA panel. Four vegetables only have the price of their product group, and six are estimates until wholesale prices from Mercamadrid are connected.
  • Edible share of each vegetable is approximate (USDA refuse factors) and will be replaced by BEDCA values. Tomato, peppers, romaine lettuce and endive are treated as eaten whole.
  • Fun facts are general knowledge and are not part of the data.
  • Illustrations come from the Vegetable Chart prototype. Page built with the Cool Graphs design system, draft 7, 13 November 2026.
