# Cool Graphs design system

Cool Graphs is a small design system for data-focused pages: charts, rankings and tables that explain what something costs or contains. It is built from the Vegetable Chart prototype, which compares 27 vegetables sold in Spain by nutritional value and price. The prototype is only the example. The system itself is not specific to vegetables.

**What is here.** Design tokens (colour in three layers: primitive, semantic and component; plus type, spacing and shape; see the Foundations pages) and twelve components: Switch, Segmented control, Button, Select, Selectable card, Disclosure, Table, Legend marker, Score bar, Banner, Chip and Icon (20 Material Design icons). Each has a story for every state. Use the Theme menu in the toolbar to view light and dark.

**Units.** Colours are hex values. Sizes and spacing are in pixels (px), and icons are 24 px by default. Contrast is the WCAG ratio, such as 4.5:1. The smallest clickable target is 30 px.

**Where the values come from.** Colours, fonts, radii and shadows were taken directly from the Vegetable Chart prototype. The accent green, the status colours (error, warning, success), the Button, the Banner, the icons the dark table header and the soft border were added afterwards. Icons are from Material Design, licensed under Apache 2.0. Every text colour is at least 4.5:1 against its background in both themes, and every icon or border colour is at least 3:1, measured with the standard WCAG formula.

**Status.** Draft 7. The components are not yet used in a live product, and the scatter chart is not part of the system. The written rules are in `DESIGN.md` in the repository.
